Kanso — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Kanso is a simple task and reminder application. This Privacy Policy explains how Kanso handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

Kanso is designed to work locally on your device. Tasks, task details, due dates, and reminder settings are stored locally on your device.

Kanso does not require you to create an account or provide your name, phone number, or email address to use the core app.

2. How Your Data Is Used

The information you enter into Kanso is used to provide task management, calendar, reminder, and notification features.

3. Local Storage

Kanso uses local device storage for app data. Your task data is not intentionally uploaded to a Kanso server by the app.

4. Notifications

If you enable reminders, Kanso may schedule local notifications on your device. Notification permission is controlled by your Android device settings. Kanso uses notification information only to provide the reminder functionality you request.

5. Sharing of Information

Kanso does not sell your personal information. Kanso does not intentionally share your task data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

6. Advertising and Analytics

Kanso does not intentionally use your task data for advertising or behavioral profiling. If a future version introduces third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or other services that process user data, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when those services are introduced.

7. Data Deletion

You can remove your tasks and other locally stored Kanso data using the features provided by the app or by uninstalling Kanso from your device. Uninstalling the app may permanently remove locally stored app data unless Android or your device provides a backup or restore mechanism.

8. Children's Privacy

Kanso is not specifically directed at children under 13. Kanso does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Security

Because Kanso stores core task data locally on your device, the security of that data also depends on the security of your device and its operating system. We recommend using the security features provided by your device.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Kanso's features or data practices change. The updated version will be published at this URL with a revised “Last updated” date.
